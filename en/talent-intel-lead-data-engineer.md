# Talent market map for a Lead Data Engineer in consulting

November 19, 2026. Research conducted in Codex using the [Talent Intel instructions](https://github.com/SriniGundelli/talent-intel/blob/main/skills/talent-intel/SKILL.md).

Data consulting for international clients. Full-time remote B2B contract. The role suits a senior or lead engineer with 5+ years of experience who has made architecture decisions independently and defended them with clients. The stack includes SQL, Python, BigQuery and Airflow 2. The role also requires English at B2 or above and experience using AI coding tools on a real project. The original Talent Intel prompt used Claude Code as an example. Any AI coding tool qualifies for the exercise.

## Companies to source from

- **Future Mind (Poland).** The team built a data platform for a client in Northern Europe using BigQuery, Dataflow and Cloud Composer. The job ad lists SQL, Python and client work. [Job ad](https://jobhunt.pl/oferta/remote-experienced-data-engineer-with-gcp-future-mind-7917a8)
- **Datatonic.** The team moved a client warehouse from Netezza to BigQuery. The case study covers the data model, ETL and training for the client's team. [Source](https://cms.datatonic.com/app/uploads/2020/09/Datatonic-On-Prem-to-Cloud-Data-Warehouse-Migration-Whitepaper.pdf)
- **Devoteam.** The team redesigned the application and data flow architecture for the e5 retail chain. The client brought customer, product and business data together in BigQuery. [Source](https://cloud.google.com/customers/e5)
- **DoiT.** The team rebuilt a Google Cloud pipeline for Huq using Dataflow, BigQuery and Cloud Composer. [Source](https://www.doit.com/customers/huq)
- **Thoughtworks.** The team modernized an airline's data platform. Consider it an adjacent pool for client architecture experience and verify BigQuery separately. [Source](https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-us/clients/data-platform-modernization)
- **Searce.** The team moved Cardinal Group's warehouse from PostgreSQL to BigQuery, adapted the SQL and checked data integrity. [Source](https://cloud.google.com/customers/cardinal-group)

## Where else to look

- **BigQuery consultants.** Datatonic, Devoteam and Searce have published examples of client data platform migrations. Ask which decisions the individual engineer owned.
- **Apache Airflow Meetups.** The project publishes a community program. Talks about production pipelines can point to orchestration work and architecture decisions. [Apache Airflow community](https://airflow.apache.org/meetups/)

## Compensation

The sample budget is up to EUR 4,000 per month on a B2B contract, before personal taxes and excluding VAT. The ranges below came from job ads found by the date of the meetup. Conversions use the November 18, 2026 rate of PLN 4.4118 per euro, rounded to the nearest EUR 10. [ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/shared/pdf/eurofxref.pdf?6dc32dd2a194e9179c83c50f19f2bc1e=)

- **Future Mind, Data Engineer (mid-level).** PLN 14,000-22,000, approximately EUR 3,170-4,990 per month. [Published range](https://www.atlantisjobs.pl/praca/data/experienced-data-engineer-with-gcp-11920)
- **Datumo, Cloud Data Engineer (mid-level/senior).** PLN 20,000-30,000, approximately EUR 4,530-6,800 per month. The bottom of the range is about EUR 530 above the sample budget. [Job ad](https://www.datumo.io/job-offers/data-engineer-gcp)

## Where to start

Start with Future Mind. It has a client project on the relevant stack, and the budget falls within the published range for an adjacent role. Look among current and former engineers for senior or lead candidates who chose the architecture and defended it with a client. Share the budget ceiling in the first message. If candidates decline because of pay, take their feedback to the hiring manager.

## Limits

Future Mind's lead rate is unknown. Its published range is for a mid-level role. Verify each engineer's experience, their use of AI for coding and their rate expectations separately. The search found no comparable public B2B rate for Datatonic, Devoteam or DoiT.
