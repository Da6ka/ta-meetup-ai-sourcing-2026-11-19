# How I teach AI to source candidates

Daria Ivanova's workshop materials for SPb IT Recruiter Meetup, November 19, 2026. Use a Lead Data Engineer brief to build LinkedIn and Google searches, test your query on sample profiles in Codex, and save your checks as a reusable sourcing skill.

1. Start with the [workshop slides (PDF)](slides.pdf) or the [editable PowerPoint](slides.pptx), and open the [two-sided A4 handout (PDF)](handout.pdf).
2. Open the [sample job description](lead-data-engineer.md), the [Talent Intel market map (PDF)](talent-intel-lead-data-engineer.pdf) and [Sasha's sample resume](sasha.md).
3. Copy the [Codex demo prompts](participant-guide.md#codex-demo), then [try the exercise on your own role](participant-guide.md#my-first-sourcing-skill).

The [participant guide](participant-guide.md) includes a hiring brief template, example searches and source links. Pay figures in the [market map](talent-intel-lead-data-engineer.md) reflect the research available on the meetup date.
