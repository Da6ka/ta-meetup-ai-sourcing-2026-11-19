# Lead Data Engineer

Sample job description for the workshop

## The role

Our consulting team designs and launches data warehouses for international clients. The lead owns warehouse architecture on 2-3 client projects. You'll learn what each client needs, choose a data model, explain your decision and take the solution through launch.

## What you'll do

Design warehouses in BigQuery and decide how data reaches them and gets updated. Write SQL and Python, and set up pipeline orchestration in Airflow. Discuss architecture decisions with clients in English.

## What we're looking for

At least 5 years in data engineering. A Senior Data Engineer who has made architecture decisions independently can also fit. Bring a project example where you can explain which model you chose, why, and what you personally owned.

The stack is SQL, Python, BigQuery, and Airflow 2 or equivalent. You'll need experience using AI coding tools on a real project and English at B2 or above.

## Contract

Full-time, remote, B2B. Up to EUR 4,000 per month before personal taxes.
